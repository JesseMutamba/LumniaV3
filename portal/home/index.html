Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Does my data need to be clean already?

No. Lumnia can detect tables and prepare common spreadsheet layouts. It surfaces uncertain mappings, missing values and inconsistencies for review. Complex files may need you to confirm the measures and periods before analysis.

Will every upload get a financial review?

The output follows the data. Sales and other tabular datasets open in Analytics Studio. Compatible financial plans and operating results can produce a financial review with comparisons, cost measures and scenarios. Lumnia flags gaps when the data does not support a result.

What can I ask my dashboard?

Start with the suggested questions in your workspace. You can explore supported measures, add breakdowns and, in a financial review, change supported scenario assumptions. Available questions depend on the data and analysis you have opened.

Are published reviews public?

Publishing a financial review saves a fixed version in your private Reports tab. Your saved, editable review remains available in Studio. The public demo uses synthetic workbooks in the same dashboard interface.

How do I get started?

If you have a Lumnia account, sign in to Studio and upload your files. If you are new, request access so we can help set up your workspace.
